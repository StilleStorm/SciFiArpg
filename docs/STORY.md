# Storyline: the case file (proposal for the player, 2026-10-18; full campaign, Diablo hours)

The campaign is one ticket, **Zgłoszenie nr 48213**: a personal application about the hero's
own status that the Komisja keeps passing to the next competent unit. Behind the units a
**cosmic phenomenon** is quietly replacing the galaxy with its most probable version; the
Komisja has it on file as **Zjawisko (sygn. 1)** and is drafting a law to make it unlawful in
about twenty years. The applicant's case is too improbable to be averaged, so the personal
ticket turns into the galaxy's errand: notify the Zjawisko, then stop it. The galaxy is
grateful; the Komisja is not, because the Zjawisko had been informed of the deadline and was
expected to wait, and now nineteen years of paperwork are void. **Five acts, sixteen planets**,
each the size of a Diablo act's quest chain: **pisma** (mandatory runs), **pisma dodatkowe**
(side tickets), a boss run, **załączniki** (dungeon chains) inside runs. Target: **16–20 hours
of mandatory campaign, 22–26 with side tickets** (§4). Five document worlds, six zone types,
six families with about thirty species, nine new clauses, thirteen new bosses, about 112
runs. Accepted parts move into DESIGN as §4.2; until then nothing here is built. Open
questions in §14. Polish samples are first drafts for the voice editor.

## 1. The premise and the plot in one page
**The personal case.** The character filed an application about their own status (§2). The
Komisja received it, found it incomplete, passed it on. Each planet is the seat of the unit
that now holds the file; each unit has its own regulamin (the chapter's clause), its own
dominant phenomenon (the family mix) and a head of unit (the boss) whose Spór comments are
the unit's position. Every pismo is one letter in the correspondence: the unit asks for
something, the applicant walks out onto the pages and gets it. The ticket number never
changes; the suffix counts the handovers (`48213/1` … `48213/16`).

**The Zjawisko.** Something has come in from beyond the margin of the printed galaxy. It does
not destroy anything. It replaces each thing with the version most users would expect: a
planet becomes the typical planet, a clerk the typical clerk, a file the most probable file.
Where it has passed, ink turns grey, lines lose their wobble, footnotes say only „Napotkano
przeciwnika”, items are called „Przedmiot (typowy)”. The Wygenerowane are its advance party
(things that exist to the degree users agree). It never answers; asked anything, it returns
the average of all answers ever given. The Komisja registered it nineteen years ago under
signature 1, drafted „Ustawa o zakazie zjawisk o charakterze kosmicznym” with a twenty-year
legislative path, and served the Zjawisko a notice of the planned date. The notice was
delivered (odbiorcy nie zastano; awizo left).

**Why the applicant.** The Zjawisko digests files by averaging them. The hero's file cannot be
averaged: a washing machine applying for personhood, a cosmonaut who arrived before departing,
a pancake that is a dwarf planet pending decision. There is no probable version of it. By act
IV it is the last unaveraged file in the Komisja's archive, and the Komisja, needing a courier
whose file the Zjawisko cannot swallow, attaches the applicant to the number and sends them to
serve a second notice (doręczenie zastępcze). The applicant does more than serve it.

**The one inexplicable thing** (DESIGN §0): the number travels ahead of the applicant. On every
planet the Asystent finds that 48213 was registered there before the character arrived. The
cause is the Zjawisko: the probable version of the applicant, the number without the person,
precedes them everywhere. The wariant struck in chapter 3 was that version. In act V the hero
meets it in full.

**The ending.** The Zjawisko is stopped (chapter 15). Planets return to their improbable
selves; thank-you letters and five-star reviews fill the notice board. The Komisja convenes
(chapter 16) and is displeased: the Zjawisko was informed of the date and was expected to wait;
the draft law, nineteen years of consultations, 48213 pages of files are now without object;
the applicant acted without legal basis. The verdict on the personal application: none
(„Rozstrzygnięcia nie wydano. Zgłaszający pozostaje w stanie, w jakim się zgłosił.”). A new
case is opened against the applicant: „postępowanie wyjaśniające w sprawie zniesienia zjawiska
bez podstawy prawnej”, number 48214. That case is the Zgłoszenia dodatkowe: the endless
post-campaign tickets are the Komisja asking you to explain yourself, planet by planet.

## 2. Per-hero subject lines (the only hero-specific story text)
Printed as "Dotyczy" on the ticket header and in the first footnote of chapter 1.
- Pralka Emancypowana: „Wniosek o uznanie za osobę. Załącznik: instrukcja obsługi.”
- Konsultant Infolinii: „Zgłoszenie opuszczenia kolejki bez wywołania numeru.”
- Pętlowiec: „Wniosek o ustalenie kolejności przybycia i wyjazdu.”
- Kartograf: „Wniosek o wpis planet do rejestru planet. Planety w załączeniu nie istnieją.”
- Naleśnik: „Wniosek o status planety karłowatej (odłożony). Prośba o informację o terminie.”
A new hero adds one line (`npm run new-hero` checklist, PLAN 8). Optionally one extra line per
hero per act (§14 Q7). In chapter 12 the Audyt reads the line out and stamps it
NIEPRAWDOPODOBNE: that stamp is what makes the hero the courier.

## 3. The chapter structure (what makes a planet an act's worth of play)
- **The base is the town.** The planet's base page (DESIGN §4.1) is the hub for the whole
  chapter. The desk offers the next pismo, the open pisma dodatkowe, and the boss run once the
  mandatory pisma are done. Tunnel, kiosk, board and paczkomat work as today.
- **Pismo** (a mandatory run, Diablo's quest). The unit writes: „Wezwanie do uzupełnienia:
  proszę dostarczyć …”. The desk stamps it, the applicant walks out through the exit into a run
  of 5–8 pages generated for that pismo (own seed, the chapter's clause and world, the family
  mix with the pismo's emphasis, a zone sequence, §3.3). One **target** from five primitives
  (§3.1) ends it with a stamp WYKONANO; the last exit is then „Zwrot do bazy”. Four per
  chapter, in order.
- **Pismo dodatkowe** (optional side ticket). Offered from the chapter's second pismo on, two
  per chapter, 4–6 pages, with a random clause from the pool („aneks do regulaminu”) and a
  guaranteed reward: a teczka of the chapter's tier +1, a certificate, or a unique on the last
  one of each act.
- **Boss run.** 6–8 pages and the boss page. Opens when the four pisma are stamped.
- **Załącznik** (dungeon). A side exit on a page (a smaller folded corner labelled „zał. 1”)
  leads into 2–3 small dense pages (about 2×2 screens, more elites, no clause clock) ending with
  a teczka and a return exit. One per pismo run, two per boss run. Optional.
- **Page events** (PLAN §8), elite packs and named elites populate every run.
- **Waypoints.** Pages reached are bookmarked; a pismo resumes at its page from the desk. The
  chapter's stamps are saved with the character.

### 3.1 Target primitives (czynności)
1. **Odbiór**: find a drawn object on a named page (teczka, parcel, stamp) and tap it.
2. **Skreślenie**: kill a named elite (PLAN §8) with its pack.
3. **Stawiennictwo**: stand on a drawn spot for a timed wave.
4. **Doręczenie**: carry an object to a station on a later page; dying drops it.
5. **Oświadczenie**: answer one Spór-style comment at a counter (outside a boss fight; a wrong
   reply costs a footnote and a retry).
A pismo may chain two. The ticket header lists the chapter's pisma as checkboxes.

### 3.2 Five document worlds (Diablo's acts look different; so do ours, inside the ban list)
Every page is ink on paper; the paper, the ink, the obstacles, the props and the base change
per act, so twenty hours of pages do not look alike. Each world is a stains + obstacles + edge
+ prop set in `render/` and `world/pages`, and a parts tint for the families present.
- **Akt I. Wydruk** (today's look): cream printout, black ink, coffee rings, staples, sticky
  notes, hand-drawn QR codes. Bases: a landing office, a village, a dotted landing site.
- **Akt II. Zeszyt i paragon**: lined notebook pages and graph paper (kratka) with handwritten
  obstacles; receipt rolls as long narrow pages with perforated edges; carbon-copy pages with
  grey doubled strokes. Bases: a village of forms, a parcel field on graph paper, a town whose
  streets are receipt rolls.
- **Akt III. Faks i gazeta**: streaky fax pages with header strips and torn edges; newsprint
  with text columns as walls, classified ads as obstacles, a crossword as a grid room. Bases:
  an office hall of counters, a reviews village, a consultation town, a delivery depot.
- **Akt IV. Karton i mikrofilm**: cardboard stock with tape and stencil marks; microfilm pages
  in the night edition (dark, forced), with frame numbers. Bases: a second-instance hall, an
  audit town, the archive of shelves.
- **Akt V. Papier uśredniony**: the Zjawisko's territory. Grey wash, the wobble filter off
  (lines unnaturally regular), a faint grid, props that are the average of the earlier worlds'
  props. Bases: the margin (one blank page with a single drawn edge), the Zjawisko's surface (a
  page that is a histogram), the Komisja's hall half averaged.

### 3.3 Six zone types (Diablo's wilderness, caves and cathedrals)
A pismo run is a sequence of zones, each a page style with its own size, density and
obstacles: **pola** (open pages, 3×4 screens, today's), **korytarz** (long narrow pages, 1×6,
enemies in lines, receipts and fax strips), **sala** (one big room, 4×4, pillars drawn as stamps
or columns of text, packs in the middle), **piwnica** (dark, night edition, 2×3, short sight,
the Kartograf's map quirk matters), **archiwum** (a grid of shelves, 3×3, corridors between
them), **załącznik** (the dungeon chain, §3). Each chapter uses three or four types; a pismo
names its sequence (e.g. pola → korytarz → sala). Zgłoszenia dodatkowe draw sequences at random.

| Boss run (6–8 pages, two załączniki, boss) | 16 | 16–22 | 4.5–6 |
| **Mandatory campaign** | 80 runs | | **16–20** |
| Pismo dodatkowe (4–6 pages) | 32 | 9–12 | 5–6 |
| **Campaign with side tickets** | 112 runs | | **22–26** |
Base time (desk, kiosk, loadout, updates) adds about 10%. Compare: Diablo 2 first playthrough
15–20 h, Diablo 3 10–15 h, Diablo 4 30–40 h. After the case: free travel to any chapter at the
character's level, and the Zgłoszenia dodatkowe as the endless loop (PLAN §7). Level pacing:
about three levels per chapter, cap 50 at the Komisja; the Karta uprawnień (DESIGN §10.3) fills
across the five acts, the last capstone around chapter 14.

## 5. The acts
- **Akt I. Przyjęcie** (ch. 1–3). Wydruk. The three existing families, the three existing
  bosses. The unit receives the file, cannot tell who the applicant is, then whether they
  exist. The Zjawisko is only a dotted planet and a wariant of the hero; the Hipoteza's last
  words name it.
- **Akt II. Uzupełnienie** (ch. 4–6). Zeszyt i paragon. Forms, attachments, the fee.
  **Formularze** enter. In chapter 5 a teczka holds the Zjawisko's file: signature 1, the
  draft law, the twenty-year path, the delivered notice. The fee in chapter 6 goes to an
  account whose number is 48213.
- **Akt III. Rozpatrywanie** (ch. 7–10). Faks i gazeta. The file waits, is reviewed, consulted
  on, delivered. **Opinie** enter. The averaging shows: typical clerks, 97% bot respondents, a
  mean that is a boss. The delivered decision concerns the probable version of the case (a
  complaint about a washing machine). First grey pages appear on the delivery depot.
- **Akt IV. Instancje** (ch. 11–13). Karton i mikrofilm. The appeal, the audit, the archive.
  **Uśrednione** enter: the Zjawisko's own things. The audit finds every file in the galaxy
  averaged except one; the archive is where the Zjawisko feeds. The Komisja attaches the
  applicant to the number and orders the second notice served.
- **Akt V. Zjawisko** (ch. 14–16). Papier uśredniony. To the margin, onto the Zjawisko, back to
  the Komisja. The applicant meets their probable version, serves the notice, does not wait,
  and is reprimanded.

## 6. Enemy variety (Diablo has thirty monster types per game; so do we)
Six families, each with four to six **species**: a silhouette (its own parts set, still
procedurally assembled) and a behaviour archetype (tank, charger, shooter, spawner, shielder,
runner, buffer). Each act tints the parts of the families present (act II forms are
hand-ruled, act IV devices are stencilled on cardboard, act V everything is grey and smooth).
Elites carry affixes from the pool of 10 (PLAN §8); named elites per pismo.
- **Urządzenia** (U): Lodówka (tank, blocks), Odkurzacz (charger), Router (shooter: signal
  arcs), Drukarka (spawner: prints Powiadomienia, later Formularze), Paczkomat (shielder, pack
  leader), **Aktualizacja** (a progress bar on legs: buffs the pack as it fills, at 100%
  installs onto the nearest enemy; harmless under 30%; claims „Pozostało: 1 minuta”, „Nie
  wyłączaj urządzenia”). Resist regulaminowy, weak egzystencjalny; defects as today.
- **Powiadomienia** (P): the four tiers as today (escalation), plus Baner (a wall that walks,
  shielder), Popup (blinks in next to the hero, charger), Telegram (runner: flees and calls
  others). Resist mechaniczny, weak regulaminowy.
- **Wygenerowane** (W): Smok kropkowany, Nogi, Niedźwiedź ewentualny, Dłoń siedmiopalczasta
  (today), plus Twarz (an averaged face with too many teeth; its stare **blokuje**) and Tekst
  (a lorem-ipsum worm, long, shooter). Existence coefficient as today.
- **Formularze** (F; from ch. 4): Arkusz (A4 sheet, basic), Rozkładówka (two-page spread,
  tank), PDF zablokowany (padlock, shielder), Rolka (receipt roll, runner that leaves a paper
  trail obstacle), Zszywka (a staple elite that pins). Trait **pole obowiązkowe**: one empty
  field with a label; the label is the weakness (Podpis = mechaniczny, Podstawa prawna =
  regulaminowy, Oświadczenie = egzystencjalny) and the form cannot fall below 1 racja until it
  takes a hit of that type. Resist egzystencjalny. Claims: „Pole obowiązkowe”, „Nieprawidłowy
  format”, „Sesja wygasła”, „Załącznik przekracza 2 MB”. Sound: rustle, pen scratch.
- **Opinie** (O; from ch. 8): Pięć gwiazdek (strong), Jedna gwiazdka (elite *z pretensją*,
  buffer), Kciuk (charger), Zweryfikowany zakup (badge, shielder), Trzy kropki (a bubble typing:
  spawner). Trait **gwiazdki**: the rating is the tier; killing a 5-star within 120 units of
  others lowers each by one star; the 1-star's death claim pulls a Powiadomienie. Resist
  regulaminowy, weak egzystencjalny. Claims: „Polecam”, „Nie polecam”, „Pomocna dla 3 osób”,
  „Zweryfikowany zakup”, „Nigdy więcej”. Sound: a plucked ding, a cursor click.
- **Uśrednione** (Ś; from ch. 12, the Zjawisko's family): grey, smooth, no wobble. Typowy
  przeciwnik (the average of the five other families' silhouettes, basic), Użytkownik typowy (a
  stick figure with the average face, charger), Wersja prawdopodobna (copies the nearest enemy
  as a grey version, spawner), Mediana (a horizontal line that cuts across the page, shooter),
  Odchylenie (the elite: the only one drawn in black, erratic, buffs by making others less
  typical). Trait **uśrednianie**: an Uśredniony near the hero for 3 s pulls one of the hero's
  stats 10% toward the hero's own mean stat (shown as a grey wash on the HUD field; recovers
  out of combat). Resist: all three equally (0.5), weak to nothing; instead every **crit** on
  them is ×3 (an improbable hit is what they cannot absorb). Claims: „Większość użytkowników”,
  „Typowy wynik”, „Nie dotyczy”, „Zgodnie z oczekiwaniami”. Sound: white noise shaped into a
  tone, a stamp with no transient. Boss: Zjawisko (ch. 15).

### Akt II. Uzupełnienie (Zeszyt i paragon)

  elite („Aktualizacja Obowiązkowa”, the Urządzenia species).

## 9. The roster screen as the frame (PLAN §7)
The first screen after the title is the **Kartoteka**: the Komisja's cabinet of applicants.

stamp. The shared stash is the „Archiwum zakładowe”, a cabinet drawer at the base. A folder
whose case is closed carries the grey stamp BEZ ROZSTRZYGNIĘCIA and the red one 48214.

- Four base layouts (urząd, archiwum, margines, powierzchnia): one slice.
- Thirteen bosses, two per slice where they share a mechanic: Wniosek + Kolejka · Załącznik +

The game is playable at every step: the desk offers the chapters and pisma that exist, and the
Zgłoszenia dodatkowe after the last built one.

## 12. Difficulty, pacing
Akt I is today's enemies with four times today's content per planet and two new species per
family. Chapter 4 brings a family and the first new boss, chapter 6 the first three-family mix,
chapter 8 the second family, chapter 12 the third; pages per run grow 5 → 8. Each act is 3–5
hours at Normalny; the whole case 16–20 hours mandatory, then free travel and the postępowanie
wyjaśniające at the character's level (max-level sets are farmed there, PLAN §7).

## 13. Voice notes for the writer
The Asystent never says the Komisja is wrong, and never calls the Zjawisko a danger: it is a
phenomenon with a signature. Units pass the file on because the regulamin requires it, never
from spite; every pismo is a request a clerk would consider reasonable. The averaging is shown,
not described: shorter footnotes, grey ink, „(typowy)”, a clerk who answers „nie mam zdania”.
The hero's heroism is never named; the Komisja names it „działanie bez podstawy prawnej” and
the galaxy names it in five-star reviews the Asystent reads out flatly. Bosses speak like users
in a comment section, the Zjawisko like a search result, the Komisja like minutes of a meeting.
